## Interpretation
The main limitation is **the few-shot episodic architecture** rather than data: strong classical models (XGBoost, RF) achieve AUROC 0.74–0.76 on the same 2,000-patient sample, while **plain episodic ETHOS+Few-Shot** (Exp1-style) often lands **below** that ceiling. A **separate pipeline** (`exp_fewshot_best.py`) combines few-shot representations with **stacking / teacher (RF)** and can approach the tabular ceiling — see **`exp_fewshot_best.json`** and the thesis discussion hierarchy (ceiling → hybrid → few-shot best → federated).
