\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discuss}

\subsection{Representation trade-offs}
Ablations (thesis Exp4) show intensity weighting matters and that bypassing tokens can increase AUROC in some configurations---honest reporting of such tensions strengthens scientific credibility.

\subsection{Data scale}
$n{=}2{,}000$ supports method comparison but limits transformer capacity; larger ICU corpora may change relative rankings~\citep{purushotham2018benchmarking}.

\subsection{Limitations}
Single-center retrospective data; simplified outcome; stacking meta-learner fit on finite validation data; MCC for few-shot-only near zero reflects thresholding in export.

\subsection{Future work}
Federated training of stacked models, multimodal notes + labs, and prospective validation.
