\subsection{Interpretation}
The small gap suggests that, for this architecture and partition, FedAvg does not catastrophically harm few-shot training. However, \textbf{absolute} performance remains far below tuned Random Forest (\textbf{0.762} AUROC in Exp1), so FL should be viewed as a \textbf{training modality} comparison, not a claim of clinical optimality.

\subsection{Node imbalance}
Stratified sampling balances nodes at cohort creation; real hospitals would exhibit uneven volumes. Extending the simulation with power-law client sizes is future work.

\subsection{Context: centralized tabular and stacked hybrids (same cohort)}
For the \emph{same} MIMIC-IV benchmark, centralized \textbf{tabular} models reach substantially higher AUROC than the few-shot models used in Exp3.
