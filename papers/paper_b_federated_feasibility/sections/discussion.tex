\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discuss}

\subsection{Simulation fidelity}
Diagnosis nodes induce meaningful covariate shift but omit legal data silos, EHR schema mismatch, and asynchronous participation~\citep{li2020federated}. Results should be cited as \textbf{proof-of-concept} for FL optimization stability, not for multi-site generalization.

\subsection{Privacy}
Update vectors can leak information~\citep{kairouz2021advances}; DP-FedAvg and secure aggregation are standard mitigations not evaluated here.

\subsection{Clinical implications}
Low absolute AUROC in the few-shot federated setting implies deployment would be inappropriate without architectural changes (e.g., federated training of \emph{tabular} models) or much larger effective sample sizes.

\subsection{Relation to companion work}
A companion manuscript details few-shot engineering and stacking that reach $\approx 0.75$ AUROC \emph{centrally}; extending those recipes to FL (and comparing to federated XGBoost) is an open engineering task.
