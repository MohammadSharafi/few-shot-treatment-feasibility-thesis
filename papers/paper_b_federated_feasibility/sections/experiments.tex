\section{Experimental setup}
\label{sec:exp}

\subsection{Software and reproducibility}
Python~3.10+, PyTorch, custom FedAvg loop; seed 42; exports in \texttt{results/tables/exp3\_results.csv}.

\subsection{Threat model (informal)}
We assume honest-but-curious servers and clients; no differential privacy noise is added in this experiment. Secure aggregation and encryption are important production extensions~\citep{kairouz2021advances}.

\subsection{Baselines beyond Exp3}
For context, centralized \textbf{tabular} models reach AUROC $\approx 0.74$--$0.75$ on the same cohort (companion paper / thesis). FL research should report both \emph{within few-shot} and \emph{vs.\ clinical ceiling} perspectives.
