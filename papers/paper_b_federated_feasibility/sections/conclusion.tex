\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

We evaluated FedAvg-style federated training of a prototypical few-shot ICU feasibility model on a five-node MIMIC-IV simulation. The AUROC drop vs.\ centralized training is \textbf{0.005}, supporting the hypothesis that privacy-preserving aggregation can be cheap in this controlled setting. Real-world FL for ICU prediction will require stronger baselines, security mechanisms, and prospective validation.

\textbf{Data/code:} PhysioNet MIMIC-IV; thesis repository for scripts and CSVs.
