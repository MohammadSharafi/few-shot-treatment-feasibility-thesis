\subsection{Synthesis}
The \textbf{stacked} few-shot pipeline (\textbf{0.746}) aligns with the strongest tabular export (\textbf{0.747}), while the \textbf{Exp1} episodic model (\textbf{0.611}) illustrates the cost of a simpler harness. Manuscripts must specify \emph{which} pipeline they report.
