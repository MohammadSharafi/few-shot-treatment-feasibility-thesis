\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

We presented a reproducible MIMIC-IV benchmark for ICU treatment feasibility with transparent separation between tabular ceilings, engineered stacked few-shot systems, single-script episodic baselines, and federated sensitivity analyses. A frozen stacked configuration achieves \textbf{0.746} test AUROC, essentially matching the best exported tabular model (\textbf{0.747}) in our repository, while documenting that simpler few-shot training remains far below tuned Random Forest (\textbf{0.611} vs.\ \textbf{0.762}) under Exp1. We hope this \textbf{reporting hierarchy} helps future work avoid ambiguous comparisons in clinical few-shot research.

\textbf{Future directions:} multi-site data, prospective labels, calibrated uncertainty, federated classical baselines, and pretrained tabular foundation models~\citep{hegselmann2023tabllm}.
